## Decision contract

Normal gates are prospectively **AHMET — PREMISE REVIEW**, **AHMET — MASTER REVIEW**, **AHMET — PUBLICATION REVIEW / AUTHORIZATION**. Premise approval authorizes development of the opportunity only. Exact master and exact release authority remain downstream and separate. The owner handoff directly prints selected planned video, why a cold viewer may care, expected payoff, truth/evidence status, intended story, why independently selected, and any material risk. Alternatives remain internal durable evidence. Approve/reject/revise framing use existing decisions; framing revision returns to the same selection gate without a new state or assumption that rejected production should be polished.

Existing explicit owner policy and scoped claim/content exclusions remain distinct from ordinary premise rejection. No new topic exclusion mechanism is justified. A future deliberate restriction requires its own actual scope/instruction; learning must not infer one.

## Genuine limitations

The session/operator still does real research and judges materiality and editorial quality. Hashes prove identity, not scientific truth or viewer interest. The live discovery helper supplies and requires consideration of available editorial judgments; direct session adapters must obey the documented completeness requirement. Replay checks its stored as-of snapshot rather than imposing future judgments. The controller does not automatically infer transferable lessons, optimize taste, prove broad-audience response or classify semantically changed stories. Context will grow with decision history; no ranking service or database is warranted now. Existing conservative crash/lock recovery and platform/device unknowns are unchanged.
