## Applying existing sensitive-topic standards

Curiosity decides whether to investigate; evidence decides what may be said. A medicine's natural origin does not establish therapeutic efficacy, an anecdote does not establish a cure, and documented misconduct does not prove an unrelated suppression claim. Educational treatment history or an evidenced account of a failed theory remains eligible; unsupported advice or efficacy claims do not.

Likewise, a patent, demonstrated prototype, commercial assertion, physical-feasibility claim, interference allegation, death and causal accusation are distinct material statements. One cannot supply evidence for another just because their juxtaposition makes a compelling hook. Exact claim scope and appropriate source quality govern each inference.

Specific documented institutional relationships, ownership, influence, fraud or covert actions may be covered. Unsupported wrongdoing must not be generalized from particular actors to an ethnicity, religion, nationality or other population. This is an application of existing exact-evidence, causation, attribution and high-care standards, not a new population taxonomy or content category.

## Hook integrity and practical limits

The hook's allegation or question must preserve the evidential scope in the opening itself. Merely attaching verified claim IDs cannot make unrelated hook prose true. The package/script hashes freeze the wording actually reviewed; claim eligibility prevents unresolved assertions from passing by reference. Required comprehension/misconception review and accurate-wording policy carry the semantic responsibility. No automatic fact checker, legal clearance or guarantee of exhaustive evidence discovery is claimed.

Legacy per-claim human-decision language and earlier review helpers remain historical; WORKFLOW-V3 is current prospective orchestration authority. Genuine exceptional owner judgment does not override a failed factual invariant and does not add another normal owner gate.

Open-world discovery remains unchanged. Allegations and mysteries have no preferred domain, quota, cadence, separate pool or viral score. They compete with all other opportunities. No actual investigation of the calibration examples, topic selection, production or publication occurred during this audit. Cycle #5 remains owner-reported scheduled, with actual publication and analytics unknown; earlier uncertainties are untouched.
