# Cold-audience fascination audit — owner review

Date: 2026-10-04. Baseline: `60743f2` (clean main). Review branch: `system/cold-audience-fascination-audit`. Authority: the owner's Cold-Audience Fascination / Editorial Intelligence Audit instruction. No cycle-start authority is granted or inferred. This report evaluates existing records; its historical subject names are not recommendations or a new backlog.

Magnivis should earn attention for knowledge a cold viewer already wants, then reward that attention with meaningful understanding. Educational legitimacy, good sourcing and elegant production are necessary capabilities but insufficient reasons to spend a publishing slot. This applies equally to mechanisms, people, decisions, investigations, unfamiliar fields and quiet conceptual stories. The target is unusually good editorial judgment, not predictable virality.

**Conclusion:** retain the current architecture and three normal gates. The integrated calibration and Premise Review already implement most of this vision. The remaining demonstrated weaknesses concern session search habits and the observability of editorial reasoning. Make a small documentation-only clarification in CONTENT-INTELLIGENCE; do not change runtime code, prompts, schemas, state machines, tests, selection thresholds or historical records. These changes await owner review on the isolated branch.

## Findings against the eighteen audit questions

| Question | Finding from current implementation and actual use |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Broad discovery? | Open eligibility is real: domains are strings, classification follows discovery, and pools include history, social choice, perception, biology and physical phenomena. Actual breadth remains session-dependent. Cycle8's broad queries repeatedly begin with institutional “surprising” discovery routes, then deepen named opportunities. This is broad but not evidence of an exhaustive or reliably open-world search engine. |
| 2. Semantic diversity? | Real alternatives exist: Cycle8 compares a natural phenomenon, a human escape and a collective-choice puzzle. Cycle7 compares preservation, animal acoustics, geography and deception. No similarity blacklist exists. Domain counts cannot establish difference in information gap, story or payoff; source-route concentration can persist underneath labels. There is no justified numeric diversity threshold. |
| 3. Research interest versus publishing pull? | Current STRATEGY explicitly distinguishes legitimacy, interest and exceptional opportunity. Prospective validation requires curiosity review and sufficient opportunity/pool judgments. Paper and Chladni predate that contract. Tally Fire and Sealed Scroll show that even a complete affirmative judgment can overestimate pull. This is a demonstrated judgment limitation, not proof that another boolean would solve it. |
| 4. Independent pre-answer pull? | `honestPremise`, `coldAudienceReason` and `interestOrigin`, plus the no-polish test, represent it directly. Premise Review exposes it before production. The validator checks presence and declared sufficiency, not human desire. Sealed Scroll's anticipated scanning answer is a useful test of the reasoning, not a universal ban on foreseeable mechanisms. |
| 5. Payoff strength? | `explanatoryPayoff`, `storyRoute`, joint sufficiency guidance and preliminary inspected premise/payoff coverage already exist. Later premise alignment blocks declared material weakening without renewed review. Nonempty prose is not a semantic guarantee. The clarification asks what remains worth understanding beyond the first reveal, within these fields. |
| 6. Conversational value? | Supported by optional `shareability`, STRATEGY and the advisory prompt. Across seven reviewed Cycle6–8 selection/pool records (49 candidate entries, including reconsidered candidates), the field is always null or absent. This proves no explicit assessment was retained there, not that no one thought about it. Clarify qualitative consideration for the selected opportunity; a neutral/unknown case remains valid. No sharing score or mandatory positive outcome. |
| 7. Ease of evidence/production selecting topics? | Paper's comparison explicitly prefers visible proof and fewer empirical caveats; Chladni's rationale cites accessible institutional sources and sound/visual interplay. These are risk signals, not proof that convenience caused either choice. Both required bespoke production. Current policy rejects convenience advantage; no code ranks renderers or source access. Distinguish access difficulty from weak editorial opportunity in comparison prose. |
| 8. Science bias? | Recent completed replacement choices cluster around physical explanation, but history was also selected twice and later rejected on premise-specific grounds. No science preference exists in current selection code/prompt. Recurrent institutional search habits and familiarity plausibly bias exposure; causal attribution is unproven. Do not impose a compensating non-science quota. |
| 9. CreativeDirection too early? | The controller requires premise approval and editorial readiness before creative completion, then direction before production. Cheap conceptual feasibility is allowed. Final media, palette, duration and renderer should not earn a candidate preference. No early-direction architectural defect was found. Historical visual convergence is separately governed. |
| 10. Evidence feasibility confused with strength? | They are separate fields and requirements. An evidence-feasible eligible candidate still needs absolute opportunity and pool sufficiency. The early rationales give feasibility considerable rhetorical weight; current fields cannot detect a disguised substitution. Clarification makes the distinction explicit in fair rival comparison without weakening evidence eligibility. |
| 11. Discovery ends too early? | `chooseTopicAutonomously` preserves/resumes rounds against a total budget, and exhaustion returns `continue-discovery`, not a forced winner. Cycles6/7 replacement searches actually used continued pools. Cycle8 stopped after one broad/deepened round with meaningful rivals. No evidence proves that stopping was premature; a larger fixed pool or mandatory extra round is unjustified. |
| 12. Competitive pools? | Current records include serious rivals and insufficient opportunities, especially Cycle8. `.min(2)` provides comparison structure, not a quality guarantee. The selected candidate may receive deeper inspection than rivals; this can be appropriate, but a source-access disadvantage must not be described as weaker fascination. Existing comparison/pool prose can carry a candid stopping rationale. |
| 13. Historical calibration used correctly? | Strategy retains the wider historical contrasts. The read-only learning adapter currently supplies Sealed Scroll rejection and Interaction-Free premise acceptance; committed prospective decisions add Oklo approval. It retains owner instruction, context, reason/interpretation distinction and non-generalizations. Tally Fire is in broader Strategy/history, not the legacy adapter array. Do not call that array an exhaustive history. No domain scoring or exclusion follows. |
| 14. Extraordinary claims enter without authority? | Yes: unreviewed leads and unverified drafts can investigate an allegation; truthful `reframed` selection is allowed, selected `unsupported` framing is blocked. Preliminary premise status includes disputed and unresolved. Evidence that someone alleged an event is not evidence the event occurred. Later exact-claim verification remains independent. |
| 15. Other narrative forms? | Story route and relevant authoring fields are open; status includes hypothetical and reconstructed. Event/history alternatives appear in real pools, and Tally Fire was selected. Uncertainty can be explained through verified statements about evidence and its limits. No automatic counterfactual simulator or exhaustive adversarial investigation engine exists. The clarification applies existing distinctions to what-ifs without creating a template. |
| 16. Prompt anchoring? | Current advisory v3 has no named-topic list or domain preference and explicitly allows unknown forms. It evaluates a supplied candidate; it does not generate the live search pool. Improving only that prompt would miss the actual session pathway. Legacy science-rendering examples and repeated “surprising” institutional queries may still prime familiar opportunities; this is an exposure risk, not proof of a deterministic topic bias. |
| 17. Premise Review sufficient? | It directly presents planned premise, cold-viewer reason, payoff, truth status/caveats, story route, selection rationale and material risks, bound to the actual selection. It is autonomous selection, not an owner menu. Oklo's explicit approval addresses the contradiction and the remaining how question. No additional owner gate or handoff schema is justified. |
| 18. Mechanisms favoring respectable explainers? | Historical missing absolute cold-audience accountability was real and already remediated. Today's residual risks are familiar search routes, selection rationalization, confusing easy evidence with opportunity, and optional signals silently omitted. Schemas cannot prove taste. The smallest useful response is clearer session practice, not an architecture claiming to measure fascination. |

## Smallest remediation and its limits

Only the current operational section of [CONTENT-INTELLIGENCE](../../docs/CONTENT-INTELLIGENCE.md#session-discovery-and-comparison-practice) changes editorial guidance:

1. Assess discovery routes as well as domain labels in existing search prose. Separate the freedom to notice a lead from the authority needed to verify it. Respond to actual concentration with useful broadening/deepening, without required source categories or a prescribed search sequence.
2. Challenge the proposed winner and fairly articulate its strongest credible rival using existing comparison/payoff/pool fields. Identify an anticipated answer and the remaining reward, while allowing familiar mechanisms with exceptional consequences. Distinguish evidence-access limitations from editorial weakness. This makes existing calibration practical; it introduces no new veto field.
3. Consider conversational value for the selected opportunity in the existing optional field or prose, including truthful uncertainty. The observed all-null/absent history warrants this clarification. No positive sharing case is required, and quiet stories are not penalized.
4. Clarify application of existing evidence distinctions to counterfactual and unresolved stories. This is coverage guidance, not a claim that the current schema prohibits them.

STRATEGY remains the single editorial authority, WORKFLOW-V3 the gate authority. Their exact bytes are retained, including Strategy's historical selection bindings. No new version of the advisory prompt is needed: it already states the principal tests and does not drive session search. No runtime contracts change, so no new automated regression or subjective topic test is appropriate. Existing regressions are rerun.

The guidance does not prescribe a curiosity checklist, a preferred subject or a query bank. All supplied calibration examples remain eligible without preference, penalty or a claim that they were consumed. Entirely unfamiliar domains remain accepted by open strings and open narrative prose. An ordinary or quiet subject may earn the slot; resemblance or difference from examples grants no advantage. Serendipity requires actual exploration by the session, not another declared diversity field.

## Investigation, safety, learning and creative freedom

Extraordinary claims can lead to support, contradiction, a partly true origin, documented misconduct or genuine unresolved uncertainty. Inspect supporting and challenging evidence, provenance, source independence and alternatives as appropriate; no predetermined verdict or false balance. Allegations about specific actors require evidence at that level. Identity never supplies evidence of collective wrongdoing. A truthful investigation can be compelling even when the viral claim is false.

Medicine remains higher care. Unsupported cure, treatment-avoidance or anti-chemotherapy claims cannot become established through an exciting hook or owner premise approval. Source/claim review requires inspected located evidence sufficient for exact wording; uncertain, conflicting and unverified claims cannot automatically promote; selected hook/narration claims must be verified. Care exceptions require scoped owner judgment, and unresolved factual blockers cannot be overridden. These are enforceable provenance/state safeguards plus accountable semantic review, not an automated detector of every dangerous implication. The audit adds no medical claim or advice.

Counterfactuals must expose assumptions and distinguish established conditional consequences from estimates and speculative social outcomes. An unresolved mystery can reward attention by explaining evidence, alternatives, exclusions and the reason for uncertainty. Honest unknowns are compatible with a satisfying story. The final form follows the subject; neither diagrams nor mystery/reveal are mandatory.

Owner decision learning retains explicit reasons and non-generalizations, includes approvals as well as rejections, and validates historical provenance without imposing later feedback during replay. Direct session adapters remain responsible for considering available context; the live helper enforces consideration of supplied decisions, but text cannot prove honest application. Generalize the publishing property, never “avoid archaeology” or “prefer nuclear physics.” Actual publication analytics remain separate. Existing small historical snapshots and recent scheduling reports cannot establish domain performance or causal success factors.

Creative convergence remains an independent downstream review of actual recent approved masters and feed impression. It asks whether resemblance serves the story or convenience; it does not mandate visual variety. We inspected the existing convergence audit and contract for this system audit, without producing a new direction or claiming fresh mobile/feed QA.
