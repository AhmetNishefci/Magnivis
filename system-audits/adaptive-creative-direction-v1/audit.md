## Findings

A. Identity is correctly defined by curiosity resolved through credible understanding, global English-first faceless knowledge storytelling, evidence bindings, accessibility/provenance and human gates. STRATEGY already keeps topics open and formats subject-led. Performance correctly treats tiny, early YouTube samples as hypotheses.

B. ContentAsset owns separate angle/script/narrative/VisualPlan and narration direction. Production scene types are open slugs. Video-specific choreography and modular audio support intentional differences. The long-form brief explicitly avoids an extended Short template. There was no universal scene factory or rule mapping domains to palettes.

C. `docs/BRAND.md` nevertheless prescribed dark/blue-white visuals, named fonts and a recovery creative reference emphasizing procedural/vector graphics and synthetic narration. Preservation of checkpoint execution could be misread as a future brand mandate. CAPTIONS described its V1 visual vocabulary as the future AI boundary despite a later per-video-judgment paragraph. VIDEO-SYSTEM and CONTENT-BIBLE reinforced registered placements and scale-led storytelling. These are real guidance discrepancies with the owner's future-facing principle; reconciled without changing historical approvals.

D. `src/captions/design-system.ts` fixes 43px typography, two placements, line limits and entry/exit frames. `MagnivisCaptionRenderer.tsx` imports global typography/palette and hardcodes ice/gold highlights, rounded gradient backplate and three motions. Caption semantics/timing are reusable, but this component combines them with art direction. `src/design/tokens.ts` carries fixed historical colors/fonts/fades. `scripts/generate-narration.mjs` selects `af_heart` globally. VideoSpec narration provenance required Kokoro. ProductionPlan format was fixed 1080x1920/30fps and asset provenance restricted to procedural/Kokoro paths. Hook archetypes and legacy video pillar labels were closed lists. These constrained available execution rather than proving the choices best for each story.

E. Wood Frog and Phantom Traffic use the same caption renderer/fonts and local voice. Phantom Traffic did **not** simply copy frog graphics: it has original shaded roadway/vehicles, controlled kinematics, a 22-car circular reconstruction and membership turnover. Shared physical motion helpers, media validation and timing are appropriate engineering reuse. No evidence establishes that captions/voice caused either video's success: no Wood Frog/Phantom Traffic performance metrics are available. Owner visual approval is quality evidence, not analytics.

F. VisualPlan/narration intent covered parts of creative judgment but lacked one durable experience/thesis record, reasoned style/voice/sound choices and an explicit recent-content convergence review. Future production validation had no direction binding.

## Resolution and boundaries

CREATIVE-DIRECTION.md is the new canonical authority: constant quality/identity, adaptive execution, no domain styles, historical principle extraction and reasoned reuse. ContentAsset owns a hash-bound companion; future ProductionPlan requires it after original editorial/claim/visual checks. A provider-neutral CI proposal stage and run-envelope support reuse existing architecture. An explicit adaptive caption V2 authoring boundary avoids modifying the historical renderer. Open explicit provider/provenance/format/hook fields allow future justified choices without injecting a default style.

No historical source, caption, audio, composition, approval, artifact, cover, platform variant/profile/QA record or delivery was edited. Current Phantom Traffic pending device gate remains intact. Fonts/palettes/voice generators are not rewritten in-place: the exact historical render dependencies must remain immutable. Future render adapters must be selected/tested for a real authorized story; no speculative styling engine or TTS integration is built now.

Quality and aesthetics remain human/agent judgment rather than a numeric novelty score. Significant direction can warrant an owner gate, routine design choices do not. Master visual review, measured presentation/device QA and publication authorization remain independent.

The milestone brain validator compares all 293 captured files with the pre-edit snapshot. Permanent regression tests exclude appendable artifact/presentation registries from a universal byte freeze: later authorized measured profiles/device decisions must remain recordable. Current registry preservation is verified separately here and by the existing Phantom Traffic binding tests. Historical content/render inputs remain exact.
