TITLE
Why a Clock Can Help Find Longitude

CAPTION
A ship’s clock preserves the time at a known reference. Sailors compare it with corrected local time at the same moment. Earth’s turning connects the difference to longitude: two hours ahead means 30° east.

Harrison’s H4 helped demonstrate accurate timekeeping at sea, while astronomical methods remained useful. The clock alone does not reveal a complete geographic position.
