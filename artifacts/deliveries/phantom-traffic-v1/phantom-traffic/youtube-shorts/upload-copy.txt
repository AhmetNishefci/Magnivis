TITLE
The traffic jam that moves backward

DESCRIPTION
The cars move forward. The slow patch can move backward.

In a controlled experiment, 22 cars formed stop-and-go traffic on a circular track with no obstruction. Small speed changes can sometimes grow in dense traffic; not every jam starts this way.

Original explanatory animation and experiment reconstruction. Synthetic narration.
Research: Sugiyama et al. (2008), https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/10/3/033001
