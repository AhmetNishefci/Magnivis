TITLE
How Can a Clock Tell a Ship Where It Is?

DESCRIPTION
A clock carries reference time. A corrected local solar observation and a same-instant comparison reveal longitude—not a complete position.

See how two hours ahead becomes 30° east, and where Harrison’s H4 fits into the story. Other astronomical methods remained useful too.

Original explanatory animation and H4 reconstruction; synthetic narration.
Sources: Royal Museums Greenwich — https://www.rmg.co.uk/stories/time/what-longitude and https://www.rmg.co.uk/collections/objects/rmgc-object-79142
