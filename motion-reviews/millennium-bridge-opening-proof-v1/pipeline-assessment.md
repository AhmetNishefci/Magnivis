# Minimal reusable capability and limits

Implemented: a small pure CPU continuous-raster warp/compositor, explicit typed camera/displacement fields, deterministic semantic influence masks, frozen source-identity validation, frame-derived timing, hash-bound approval/provenance and a silent prototype-specific encode/QA command. No provider API, infrastructure, generic scene framework or full ProductionPlan is added. Existing source/raster/typography boundaries are reused.

The prototype uses authored image-space depth/parallax and localized torso/arm/foot deformation rather than independent generated frames, rigid cards or whole-image panning. Grading and depth blur remain in the original source material; adding them as new architecture is unnecessary for this limited experiment. Masks control motion influence; they are not reusable clean plates. A full scene cannot be inferred from one image.

If owner-approved, consider a small scene-local layered asset contract for accurate occlusion, clean plates, contact landmarks, bounded mesh/pose rigs and camera/layer bindings. Extract shared components only after another scene proves reuse. Larger camera moves, turning limbs, crowd animation and damping mechanics need controlled geometry or richer layered rigs. A Remotion adapter could consume the same pure frame/time models later; the CPU proof is an isolated experiment, not a replacement for the registered production renderer.

Approval of this proof would justify assessing the next production step; it would not authorize the complete film, final captions, locked master, variants or publication automatically. Meta remains separate and requires actual owner historical screenshots/evidence after master approval. Do not guess Instagram or Facebook surface coordinates.
