## Universal creative vision

**Magnivis — See the unimaginable.** Magnivis makes knowledge fascinating across the extraordinary depth of everything worth knowing. Every video should leave the viewer understanding something fascinating or useful that they did not understand before. The universe is open to legitimate subjects and fields never previously anticipated. **Discover first. Classify second.** Familiar science, historical events and remarkable true stories remain fully eligible; none defines the whole brand.

## Apply during authorized discovery and comparison

Before generating leads or comparing candidates in an owner-authorized cycle, read this supplement alongside STRATEGY and CONTENT-INTELLIGENCE, and consult the [creative inspiration library](CREATIVE-INSPIRATION.md) for its three purposes and eligibility semantics. Continue fresh open-world exploration beyond it. Project metadata is already a mandatory startup read under AGENTS; its `prospectiveEditorialGuidance` routes these instructions into the actual session pathway. The supplied-candidate advisory prompt is not the discovery engine and is unchanged.

Consciously distinguish subject matter, narrative form, curiosity structure and visual treatment when considering recent work and current opportunities. Different subjects can repeat the same narrative or curiosity pattern; similar subjects can support very different experiences. These distinctions are editorial considerations, not mandatory tags, rows, classifications, quotas or scored axes. Preliminary visual feasibility remains appropriate before Premise Review; full CreativeDirection remains downstream of premise approval and evidence verification.

Use existing `searchBreadth` and search-record prose to notice when familiar sources, query habits or lead-generation routes plausibly overlook strong opportunities. Event-focused broadening can still concentrate on incidents, danger or reversals. When warranted, explore an overlooked question, mechanism, argument, observation or connection, then seek domain-appropriate evidence. Source authority governs verification, not the freedom to notice a lead. No fixed source list, required extra search round, non-event finalist, category rotation or exhaustive coverage claim follows. Existing finite resumable discovery and adequate-pool stopping rules remain intact.

In existing `comparison`, `explanatoryPayoff`, `curiosityReview.storyRoute` and `poolReview.rationale`, give serious rivals their strongest honest case on their own terms. A worthwhile payoff can be understanding a mechanism, seeing a mathematical demonstration, resolving an everyday mystery, exploring a philosophical argument, recognizing a connection, understanding scientific uncertainty or experiencing a perspective-changing explanation. These are examples, not a closed list or equal-value guarantee. Human danger, historical incidents, multiple twists, dramatic rescues and definitive causal resolutions are not requirements. Nor do alternative structures receive automatic preference. Judge actual cold-audience pull and worthwhile understanding together, preserving the absolute opportunity standard.

Do not downgrade a proof, demonstration, argument or uncertainty explanation merely because it lacks another plot revelation or cannot imitate an event story. Equally, do not invent fascination for a weak explanation in the name of variety. Keep evidence-access limitations and production feasibility separate from intrinsic merit. Available renderer/assets, recent selections and owner examples confer no convenience advantage. A great event story can still win; an unfamiliar opportunity unlike every library entry can win on its own merit.

Library concepts are legitimate exact candidates, inspiration and taste calibration. Mention or prior evaluation does not consume them. Reconsider them fairly alongside retained leads and completely new opportunities, without automatic inclusion in every pool, selection preference, avoidance penalty, semantic similarity target or ordered queue. Actual prior Magnivis story coverage remains the repetition consideration. Register a candidate only when the normal authorized discovery work actually considers it; this library registers none.

## Truth, creative experience and learning

Existing evidence standards remain non-overridable. Science separates observations, models, assumptions and speculation; counterfactuals define conditions and supported consequences; history separates events, interpretation and reconstruction; psychology preserves study limitations; medicine avoids unsupported health claims; philosophy distinguishes arguments from empirical findings. Owner-endorsed premises are unverified until evidence inspection, and neither premise approval nor this calibration verifies claims.

Let the subject determine its strongest truthful experience. Compelling openings, a reason to care, meaningful progression, clear explanation, purposeful visuals, natural narration, thoughtful sound and a memorable payoff remain creative goals rather than a rigid score or scene formula. Earn runtime. Apply the existing [openings, endings and selective engagement guidance](CREATIVE-DIRECTION-OPENINGS-ENDINGS.md) alongside CREATIVE-DIRECTION and PRODUCTION-PLANS before direction and rendered editorial review. The current owner approval reaffirms that supplement; no historical master is changed.

Use [ANALYTICS](ANALYTICS.md) and the existing performance context with native definitions, windows, mappings and limitations preserved. The V2 CSV intake contains real exports; missing capture metadata, ambiguous exact-media mappings, sparse observations and uninspected TikTok panels still prevent causal or cross-platform equivalence claims. Improve openings, comprehension, pacing and satisfying explanation through cautious learning; do not narrow the long-term creative universe to short-term winners.
