# Long-form 001 brief — The True Scale of the Universe

Status: research and story development  
Target format: landscape 16:9  
Target runtime: approximately 8–10 minutes  
Working title only: **The True Scale of the Universe**

## Viewer promise

Build one continuous mental model from human scale to the observable universe. Each chapter must preserve a reference from the previous chapter long enough for the viewer to feel it become irrelevant. The result should feel like one escalating journey, not a compilation of disconnected size facts.

## Working story spine

1. **Human reference** — begin with a person and a familiar distance.
2. **Earth** — city, continent, circumference, and planetary diameter.
3. **Earth–Moon system** — reveal how misleading common diagrams are.
4. **Sun and planetary system** — shift from object size to orbital distance.
5. **Light-time solar system** — seconds become minutes and hours.
6. **Nearest stars** — Proxima Centauri turns the solar system into a point.
7. **Milky Way** — tens of thousands of light-years and hundreds of billions of stars, subject to source review.
8. **Local Group / Andromeda** — galaxies become neighboring objects.
9. **Observable universe** — finish on cosmic horizons, expansion, and the distinction between universe age and present comoving diameter.

The exact sequence, values, and chapter durations remain unapproved until research is complete.

## Creative requirements

- Open with a visual contradiction in the first 10 seconds; avoid a slow documentary preamble.
- Maintain a persistent scale/reference device rather than resetting the visual grammar in every chapter.
- Use narration as the connective tissue, but make the comparisons understandable without it.
- Vary camera behavior and visual treatment by scale regime so the film does not feel like an extended Short template.
- Create deliberate quiet moments; eight minutes of constant impacts and zooms will feel synthetic and exhausting.
- Design dedicated 16:9 compositions and safe areas. Do not crop or stretch Shorts scenes.

## Research workstreams

- Earth dimensions and human-scale reference values.
- Mean Earth–Moon distance and orbital variation.
- Planetary diameters and semimajor axes from NASA/JPL.
- Solar-system boundary definitions: planetary region, heliopause, Oort Cloud, and the uncertainty of each.
- Proxima Centauri distance and nearby-star context.
- Milky Way diameter, stellar population, and the uncertainty/range behind both.
- Andromeda distance and Local Group scale.
- Observable-universe age, light-travel distance, expansion, and present comoving radius/diameter.

Every production claim must enter a committed typed dataset with source, retrieval date, basis, precision, and uncertainty notes before rendering.

## Likely reusable foundations

- Existing `CelestialBody`, `BackgroundStars`, typography, safe-area, scene-window, audio-cue, render-routing, and QA systems.
- Video 004's `DistanceBeam` and `LightTunnel`, generalized only if the storyboard proves reuse.
- A landscape camera/scale system and chapter timeline built specifically for long-form needs.
- Modular narration and caption generation; long-form captions should be derived from the approved narration script.

## Production gates

1. Research dataset reviewed.
2. Thesis and chapter order approved.
3. Narration outline and animatic timing approved.
4. Landscape design frames reviewed at desktop, mobile, and TV sizes.
5. Rendering-performance test completed before the full composition is built.
6. Full narration, sound design, captions, render QA, and human review completed before private upload.

## Explicit non-goals for this phase

- No full eight-minute composition yet.
- No speculative 3D/WebGL dependency before design frames prove it is necessary.
- No third-party footage or textures without a documented license.
- No “largest,” “edge of the universe,” or other misleading language without precise definitions.
