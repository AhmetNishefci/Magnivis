# Adaptive creative direction V1

**Magnivis is a brand, not a video template.** Consistency of quality and identity; intentional creative execution. This authority applies to future content only. Wood Frog, Phantom Traffic and every historical approved asset retain their exact scripts, render inputs, approvals, artifact identities and presentation evidence. Recovery remains CLOSED.

## Constants and decisions

Brand constants are evidence-first accuracy, intellectual honesty, curiosity resolved through understanding, clarity, premium execution, strong storytelling, meaningful visual communication, intentional design, accessibility/readability, provenance safety, platform awareness and human approval gates. Magnivis remains global, faceless and English-first: **See the unimaginable.** Discover first. Classify second. Categories organize learning; they never whitelist topics or select a domain style.

Palette, fonts, caption appearance/animation, narrator delivery, sound/music/silence, pacing/duration, transitions, camera language, scene structure, hook, visual medium, information density, disclosure presentation and production technique are adaptive decisions for each ContentAsset. No science-blue/history-sepia or other domain mappings. No enforced novelty quota. Reuse is welcome when it improves this story; novelty is valuable only when it improves attention, comprehension, emotion, memorability or quality.

Ask: **What is the most compelling truthful way to experience this idea?** Reason from topic and evidence through curiosity, emotional target, story structure, central visual idea, art/medium, narration, captions, sound, pacing, transitions and platform presentation. This is a reasoning sequence, not a compulsory scene order or fixed menu of media.

## Existing boundary, new first-class record

KnowledgePackage → ContentAsset + approved script/beats/VisualPlan → **asset-bound CreativeDirection** → ProductionPlan → implementation/CaptionPlan/audio → candidate/master review → PlatformVariant → presentation-surface/device review → separately authorized publication.

The direction is a file-backed companion to the existing ContentAsset, not a new content hierarchy, topic, database or universal video factory. Keep future records under `content-intelligence/creative-directions/<asset-slug>/direction-v<n>.json`; commit the record and any review decision before its production handoff. Draft direction may inform editorial discussion, but the implemented provider workflow requires approved/verified editorial inputs. Rebind after any approved editorial revision; creative excitement cannot rewrite a locked factual qualification, script, hook, beat or VisualPlan. Follow V3’s internal editorial re-verification contract or genuine exceptional escalation instead.

`src/content-assets/creative-direction.ts` validates identity/revision, exact package/asset/script hashes, creative and visual theses, viewer experience/emotional target, decisions with story-specific rationale, convergence review, significant-owner-review assessment, risks and truthful provenance. Eight responsibilities must be evaluated: visual medium, art direction, narration, captions, sound, pacing, hook and platform presentation. Values are open text, not enum styles. Explicit none/not-applicable with a reason is legitimate; optional decisions can describe color, camera/motion, transitions, density or disclosure treatment separately or within art direction. Alternatives are useful for significant choices, not mandatory paperwork for every detail.

States are `proposal` and `ready-for-production-planning`. The latter means the direction has been considered and source-validated; it is **not** human editorial, master, platform or publication approval. AI output stays proposal; V3 permits routine internal finalization under explicit cycle-start authority without an intermediate owner decision. An agent may finalize routine creative decisions within current authorization. If a significant departure, unresolved risk or owner request warrants direction review, record the reason and exact owner-decision reference before readiness. Owner identity and instruction must come from actual authorization; use decision-entry conventions when no review timestamp was supplied. Do not ask Ahmet to choose every font, color or voice.

ProductionPlan now binds the direction ID/revision/SHA. `validateProductionPlanReferences` requires its actual record, approved source hashes, resolved convergence review and ready state. Future assets require inspectable rights/provenance evidence. Only the three **exact pre-milestone plan hashes** listed in `system-audits/adaptive-creative-direction-v1/historical-plans.json` bypass this new requirement. A reused ID or changed revision/hash cannot gain historical exemption. Do not expand exemptions to evade the future gate.

## Captions and narration

Designed burned-in captions remain part of every new narrated Short. Principles stay speech-first: semantic phrase grouping, exact narration reconstruction, intentional timing, selective emphasis, readability and composition/platform awareness. Visual execution is chosen per story: font/license, size/weight/case, cue density, placement, motion, highlighting/backplate or large typography. Some visual moments can breathe without caption presence between spoken phrases; complete narration coverage still applies. Do not remove spoken qualifiers or create caption claims independent of narration. A genuinely silent story needs an explicit accessibility/editorial treatment rather than fabricated narration.

The immutable V1 caption schema/design system/renderer is the **historical Wood Frog/Phantom Traffic treatment**, not the only permitted Magnivis treatment. `src/captions/adaptive-plan.ts` provides a separate V2 authoring/validation path with explicit direction/script binding, chosen font/appearance/animation/renderer, authored bounds and exact semantic/timing checks. No V1 fallback. Renderer implementation and typography-fit tests are chosen when a future story exists; this milestone does not implement or render a speculative new style. Canvas containment is not native-UI safety. Real-device, safe-region and readable-font checks remain required before approval.

Kokoro `af_heart` is the **current primary narrator**, explicitly selected by Ahmet during Longitude Clock candidate-v1 review. Use it by default for auditory brand continuity. Topic/domain changes do not justify routine per-video auditions. A material deviation needs an explicit rationale, such as multilingual work, character/quotation differentiation, a deliberate series, accessibility, a format-specific requirement or demonstrated quality/performance evidence. This is not an eternal voice lock: explicit owner decisions may evolve the primary narrator. Delivery, pace, pauses, energy and pronunciation remain story-specific. Record exact provider/model/voice/settings/audio identities, measure duration and review the actual mix. Paid providers and rights remain separately governed.

`src/design/brand-execution-policy.json` records the current decision; its validator and future ProductionPlan binding require narrator rationale while independently preserving adaptive visuals, captions and soundscape. Exact pre-policy plans retain their original semantics through hash-bound adoption records; changed records gain no exemption. Earlier bf_emma proposal/audition decisions remain historical evidence, not silently reinterpreted.

## Medium, sound, pacing and hooks

Choose medium for truthfulness, explanatory/emotional power, visual quality, temporal consistency, rights, feasibility and platform fit. Procedural/vector, physical simulation, photorealistic imagery, footage, archives, maps, anatomical scenes, typography, documentary/character/environmental storytelling and hybrids are examples, not a menu or domain rule. ContentAsset accepts bespoke/hybrid intent; hook archetypes and legacy VideoSpec pillar labels accept open normalized slugs without changing existing knowledge pillars; ProductionPlan scene types were already open. Its format is now explicit positive dimensions/FPS/duration, enabling future justified long-form rather than mandating vertical 30fps for every asset. Platform envelopes still validate actual destination media.

Music, ambience, effects and silence are independent story decisions. Narration intelligibility is constant. No universal soundscape, background track, music style, escalation or automatic music bed. Primary-narrator continuity never implies sonic templating. Duration is story-led. Business/distribution may record platform monetization thresholds, but threshold eligibility alone cannot require padding, and habitual runtimes cannot require shortening. Shorts and long-form are distinct strategic formats; 30 seconds, 48 seconds or over 60 seconds are legitimate when the story and supported format warrant them. Runtime and edit rhythm follow the explanation; neither weak material stretched to a quota nor qualifications compressed away. Hook treatment follows the approved story: no universal question, paradox or six-beat formula. New direction cannot silently replace an approved hook.

## Convergence and reusable engineering

**Adaptive Creative Direction** derives the strongest truthful experience from the story. **Cross-cycle convergence awareness** asks whether recent precedent, habits or tooling are quietly replacing that authority. **Forced visual diversity is not a Magnivis goal.** Similarity is not failure; do not alternate light/dark, ban repeated backgrounds/colors/fonts, rotate presets, map categories to styles, require a novelty quota, maintain a finite treatment whitelist or let a numeric visual-distance/diversity score decide the treatment.

Before a prospective direction passes source/readiness validation, its existing `convergenceReview` must include `portfolioReview` and a `visualComparison` for each of the **four latest available approved/locked masters**, or all available when fewer exist. This is a bounded inspection window, not a style quota. `recentApprovedVisualWorks` selects by actual decision-entry time at the direction's recorded entry time, excludes the same ContentAsset, deduplicates superseded masters of the same asset, and incorporates new V3 journals alongside existing legacy master-approval adapters. Publication, popularity and analytics are not selection criteria. With genuinely no predecessors, preserve the explicit reason; do not omit available recent works to avoid inconvenient comparisons. Independent journal and exact owner-decision bindings remain necessary; the existing V3 controller continues semantic replay at its gates.

Compare **actual rendered outputs**, not documentation labels alone, and read available historical CreativeDirections. Bind canonical master identity, exact approval/direction files, inspected canonical image/video evidence and a hash-bound inspection record identifying those outputs and findings. Inspect enough samples to understand opening, explanatory construction and payoff; report coverage and access limits honestly. Missing historical direction or media is a limitation, never an invented record. Sampling cannot prove continuous motion, mobile safety or native feed crops. No historical render or duplicated media is required for this comparison.

Record the overall viewer/feed impression as well as story-relevant dimensions. Background luminance/environment, dominant palette/contrast, medium/rendering language, composition/spatial organization, typography/captions, camera/motion/transitions, object/material and diagram treatment, metaphor, lighting/texture/depth, information density, opening and payoff are descriptive prompts, not mandatory rows or scored axes. Different labels, fonts or hues can still produce a similar overall impression. Record a proposed feed impression, selected comparison scope and tooling-bias check.

When similarity exists, explain its cause: **story-justified continuity**, **deliberate brand continuity**, or **convenience-driven convergence**. Multiple causes can apply. Independent story reasons and intentional brand principles—clarity, hierarchy, strong hooks, narration quality, readable speech-first captions, qualifications, pacing, polish and coherent storytelling—permit familiar execution. They do not automatically justify an inherited background, palette or renderer aesthetic.

If convenience, available renderer/assets, implementation precedent or unexamined habit drive a choice, explore a **materially different approach internally** before readiness. Record what changes, explanatory/emotional tradeoffs, resolution and why the chosen treatment serves the story independently of tooling availability. Changing only a label or a cosmetic accent is not meaningful exploration. Exploration may legitimately return to the original treatment; a justified resemblance needs no artificial differentiation. Available engineering/assets earn no creative preference solely because they exist. Unresolved convenience reuse and convenience-driven similarity without this response block production planning. No new normal owner gate is introduced; existing exceptional escalation remains unchanged.

The six exact pre-change direction records listed in `system-audits/cross-cycle-creative-convergence/historical-directions.json` retain their original semantics by normalized hash, including historical proposals. Changed revisions/content do not inherit that exemption. Approved historical media, original decisions, production inputs and independent journals are frozen; this rule is prospective and makes no retrospective failure judgment.

Reuse rendering infrastructure, timing/reconstruction utilities, safe-area/media validation, audio normalization, provenance, deterministic motion and camera/typography primitives. Reusing engineering does not silently import art direction. A legacy renderer helper carrying colors/fonts/backplates is a concrete style choice. Choose it only with rationale, or implement the smallest story-specific adapter. Do not build a universal traffic engine or caption framework before a story needs one.

## Content Intelligence, analytics and long-form

`src/content-intelligence/creative-direction.ts` maintains `workflow.creative-direction` (current prompt version 3) through the existing provider interface, schemas and provenance. It consumes approved package/asset, recent-asset snapshots and an exact visual-inspection record. The prompt includes bounded approved master identities, bound inspection findings and available historical directions. It rejects source drift/self-approval and validates the exact compared identities. The existing run-envelope schema supports its stage/derived record; serialize through existing run-store hashing, never hand-edit hashes. The legacy Wood Frog topic CLI is not extended into automatic future production. Source-review/live-stage pauses, paid-call authorization and human editorial gates remain unchanged. Manual session-assisted decisions should be labeled manual/AI-assisted, not passed off as provider runs.

A successful video is precedent, not proof its style caused success. Analytics should form separate hypotheses about topic interest, hook, first-second comprehension, rhythm, retention/payoff timing, captions, emotional reaction, sound, novelty and platform presentation. Preserve native metrics, sample size, windows and uncertainty; correlation is not causation. Compare principles and test applicability, not “copy the winner.” No metrics are invented for Wood Frog or Phantom Traffic.

Long-form inherits identity and evidence principles, not a Shorts visual grammar. Chapter structure, pace, narrator, music, typography, graphics and evidence presentation may differ. No long-form production or new content cycle is started here.
