# Strategy

Magnivis is a durable visual-science media brand, not an automated content farm.

## Portfolio roles

- Shorts (roughly 25–50 seconds): discovery, concept testing, and audience acquisition.
- Long-form (initially roughly 6–12 minutes): watch time, deeper engagement, monetization, and durable channel value.

Quality, scientific trust, and a distinct creative idea outrank production volume. Reuse primitives and workflows, not a single identical video template.

## Initial pillars

1. Scale of the universe
2. Earth
3. Humanity and engineering
4. Numbers and money
5. Technology and the future

## Production gate

The current workflow is local render → human review → manual/private upload → YouTube processing/copyright/quality review → metadata review → manual publish. Software must not bypass those gates.

## Success signals

Evaluate retention, replays, completion, comments that demonstrate understanding/awe, and long-term subscriber/viewer quality. Do not optimize for raw output count.
