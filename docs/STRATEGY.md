# Strategy

Magnivis is a durable visual-science media brand, not an automated content farm.

## Portfolio roles

- Shorts (roughly 25–50 seconds): discovery, testing topics and hooks, audience acquisition, identifying high-performing subjects, and rapid experimentation.
- Long-form (initially roughly 6–12 minutes): deeper storytelling, watch time, durable channel value, stronger viewer relationships, and monetization-oriented watch-hour growth.

Quality, scientific trust, and a distinct creative idea outrank production volume. Reuse primitives and workflows, not a single identical video template.

## Publishing ramp-up

| Phase | Publishing target | Primary objective |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | 3 premium Shorts; no long-form requirement | Validate the renderer, production workflow, visual identity, sound design, mobile readability, and overall quality. |
| Week 2 | 4 premium Shorts and 1 long-form video | Begin proving that reusable components reduce production time without lowering quality. |
| Weeks 3–4 | 5 premium Shorts and 1 premium long-form video per week | Establish the intended operating rhythm while preserving creative and scientific standards. |

After the ramp-up, the steady-state target is:

- 5 Shorts per week
- 1 long-form video per week
- approximately 20–22 Shorts per month
- approximately 4 long-form videos per month
- approximately 24–26 total videos per month

This cadence is a target, not a quota. It never overrides quality, scientific accuracy, asset rights, human review, or the requirement that each story have a genuine creative reason to exist. Do not publish filler, repetitive variants, weak research, or unfinished work to satisfy the schedule. If quality cannot be maintained, reduce cadence and document the constraint.

The architecture should progressively make this cadence feasible through reusable primitives and deterministic workflows without turning Magnivis into an AI content farm. Do not pre-build a 20-video inventory during V1. Scale production only as the workflow demonstrates that it can preserve quality.

## Initial editorial direction

Video 001 is Earth → Jupiter → Sun → giant stars. Current candidates for the next Shorts are:

1. How Deep Is the Ocean, Really?
2. What $1 Billion Actually Looks Like
3. The Largest Things Humans Have Ever Built
4. How Fast Is the Speed of Light?

The current first long-form candidate is **The True Scale of the Universe**.

These are candidates, not immutable commitments. Research, creative feasibility, and actual YouTube performance data may change their order or replace them. Use audience data increasingly as the sample becomes large enough to support decisions; do not overreact to the first few hours of a single upload.

## Production gate

The current workflow is local render → human review → manual/private upload → YouTube processing/copyright/quality review → metadata review → manual publish. Software must not bypass those gates.

## Success signals

Evaluate retention, replays, completion, comments that demonstrate understanding/awe, and long-term subscriber/viewer quality. Do not optimize for raw output count.
