## Final exact proposed narration — awaiting owner approval

How can a clock tell a ship where it is?

By keeping the time at a known reference point.

Sailors use the Sun to find local time, corrected to match the clock’s timekeeping.

Compare them at the same moment.

Earth’s turning links that time difference to longitude: fifteen degrees per hour.

Twelve at the reference. Two in the afternoon locally.

Two hours ahead. Thirty degrees east.

Harrison’s H4 helped demonstrate accurate timekeeping at sea.

Other astronomical methods remained useful too.

The clock carries time. Observation and calculation reveal longitude.

## Solar-time correction resolved

The sentence “Sailors use the Sun to find local time, corrected to match the clock’s timekeeping.” attaches correction directly to the observed reading rather than adding a detached equation-of-time digression. It means convert apparent solar time to mean solar time; it does not mean change a civil time zone, reset the reference clock or erase the local/reference difference. Required factual visual disclosure: **BOTH MEAN SOLAR TIME**. The same-moment sentence and **SAME INSTANT** disclosure make the numerical example a simultaneous comparison, not two elapsed hours of voyage.

The reference display is corrected for known clock error; day/date ambiguity is resolved. 12:00 reference and 14:00 local are illustrative mean-time readings. Neither is labeled an apparent local-noon observation. This preserves the accepted USNO/Bowditch relationship without saying “equation of time” in narration. A visual-only footnote or total deletion would leave spoken meaning less reliable; the selected solution uses both a short spoken correction and an exact conceptual factual disclosure.

“Earth’s turning links that time difference to longitude: fifteen degrees per hour” refers to the consistent solar-time-difference/longitude conversion. It does not assert exactly 360° of stellar-relative rotation in 24 SI hours. Geometry remains schematic, with solar-time basis explicit. East-positive sign is Δlongitude = 15 × (local mean hours − reference mean hours): (14 − 12) × 15 = +30°. East cannot be mirrored or reversed by later creative execution.
