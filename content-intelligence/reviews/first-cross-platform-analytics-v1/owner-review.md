### What can be learned across platforms

There is no defensible combined view total, global winner, cross-platform retention score or common engagement denominator. YouTube covers 16 posts; Meta covers 11; TikTok examples are a selected subset with no counts. Windows, ages, identities and distribution differ.

Hotter Pan's reported Instagram concentration and TikTok viewing weakness can coexist. A worthwhile topic may receive different audiences and distributions or have an execution weakness. That contrast argues for platform-specific diagnosis while retaining the creative premise as a hypothesis. “Good topic” and “good opening” are separate judgments; neither excuses a weak payoff.

Audience anticipation is not measured by these exports. Returning viewers, repeat visits, repeat consumption across posts and comments expressing understanding are better future evidence, where native tools provide them. A single save/follow or a replay-prone view count is not a loyalty measurement.

## 7. Topic, hook, story, visual, audio and pacing recommendations

### Discovery and hooks

Apply existing cold-audience pull + worthwhile payoff judgment to an open search. Look for consequences, hidden relationships, striking true decisions, practical understanding and visualizable uncertainty; pursue unfamiliar leads, then seek appropriate evidence. Ask what someone would want to explain to another person, including material caveats. Do not prefer science, history, familiar institutional routes, ready renderers or a recent winner's domain.

When worthwhile, preserve substantively different hook candidates in existing package/editorial records: a contradiction, consequential choice, visible demonstration or concrete scale comparison are examples, not quotas. Compare what each makes comprehensible, which unresolved question survives, its independent truth, opening visual feasibility and how soon relevance is apparent. Reject near-synonym batches and generic “Did you know?” unless a specific treatment earns it. A strong declarative opener is fully eligible.

### Narrative and endings

Choose the form after understanding the story: counterintuitive explanation, extraordinary true story, scale revelation, everyday mystery, mechanism demonstration, suspense, thought experiment, psychological finding, myth/evidence or practical model can each work. Keep one central promise and one satisfying resolution; intermediate reveals earn their place by changing expectation or supplying necessary understanding. Uncertainty itself can be the payoff when evidence warrants it.

Before drafting the ending, express the intended retelling in one plain sentence. Test whether it remains accurate without the video. This is a proposed session exercise inside existing payoff/comprehension prose, not a new compulsory field. End at the conceptual resolution when appropriate. Avoid artificial cliffhangers, rote “follow for more” or a long logo. Anticipation comes from consistently rewarding attention, not withholding the answer. Save/share value is a hypothesis to observe, never a CTA requirement.

### Visual development

For each consequential beat, specify the change a viewer can see and the claim it clarifies. Carry object identity, time, scale, attribution or evidence status across views when useful. Use original 2D for clean transformations; projected depth/selective 3D for occlusion, layers, camera or spatial relationships; maps/timelines for where/when; licensed archives for evidence/context; typography for genuinely textual stakes. Don't add all media to every story. Archival stillness can be purposeful; constant motion can impede understanding.

Reuse geometry, timing, reveal, safe-area, typography measurement and audio utilities; keep story data separate from primitives. Current Remotion/SVG/CSS and story-specific depth are sufficient for the next investigation. No demonstrated need for a new 3D engine, GPU stack or asset-generation service. Any reconstruction must remain labeled and source-faithful; scientific diagrams cannot silently imply measurement, and archival imagery is not automatically licensed because accessible.

Keep designed burned-in captions as part of the new master. Favor complete spoken phrases with selective semantic emphasis, honest punctuation and exact reconstruction. Let visual explanations breathe between speech, while preserving qualification coverage. Phone-scale decoded inspection and actual native device QA have different evidentiary strength; caption midpoints, covers, motion and surfaces remain separately bound. Never invent current platform UI/crop geometry.

### Narration and sound

Retain primary Kokoro `af_heart`. There is no evidence here that replacing it would improve audience outcomes. Evaluate delivery rather than routine voice auditions: complete sentence generation, supported pronunciation surfaces, semantic stress, conversational clauses, intentional pauses and transitions into reveals. Captions must not determine TTS synthesis boundaries. ASR helps detect omissions/ambiguity; it does not certify stress, emotion or naturalness.

R: Vermeer V2's independent actual-master audio report measures **−21.0 LUFS integrated**, 2.9 LU range and a reported true-peak value of −2.2 dBFS. Its mastering file also includes a normalization-analysis output of −19.03; that is not the actual final mix measurement. Use the actual-media report as evidence, not a configuration/analysis target. This does not establish that the narration is too quiet on platforms, or a universal replacement loudness target. No listening assessment is claimed here.

After an approved future script, make a short internal listening comparison with the same voice, wording and intelligibility-matched speech level: voice-led baseline versus a restrained original/appropriately licensed bed or meaningful effect. Choose sound that reveals scale, contact, change, suspense or resolution; silence is a valid deliberate choice. Listen on available phone/speaker/headphone conditions, particularly quiet clauses and qualifiers; document masking and scope. Reduce/remove competing sound before increasing voice speed or gain. Record cue/master measurements and provenance; add every actually selected production asset to ASSET-LICENSES. No unlicensed competitor audio, voice imitation, trending song or paid service.

### Duration and rhythm

Explore approximately **20–35 s**, **35–50 s**, and **longer** as editorial routes when a separately authorized story supports them. They are experiments, not ideal ranges, enforced bins or performance guarantees. A complex historical/evidence chain may earn more time; a simple result may resolve quickly. Remove duplicated setup and unnecessary terminology before accelerating speech. Keep comprehension pauses where the visual relation needs time. Do not speed up indiscriminately, cut every fixed number of seconds, pad for monetization or compress away uncertainty.

Review an edit as changing knowledge: what is newly learned, what question changes, what must remain on screen long enough? Fast visual density can conceal the mechanism. A long static shot may still earn its time. Review the complete result and caption midpoints separately from decode/geometry checks; internally fix demonstrable weaknesses before presenting exact bytes for owner judgment.
