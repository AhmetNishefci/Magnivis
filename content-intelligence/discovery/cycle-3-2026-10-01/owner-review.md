**Classification after discovery:** earth-nature; marine-biology, biological-archives. **Timing:** evergreen.

**Core question:** What can layered whale earwax reveal about a life no one watched?

**Why care:** An unlikely museum object could preserve observations across a life that researchers could not continuously watch.

**Expected payoff:** Understand how a physical sequence can become a time-indexed biological archive.

**Prior-content relationship:** Ocean Depth shares ocean context; Wood Frog shares biology. Neither reconstructs life through an archival specimen. Avoid a maritime continuation from Longitude or a survival spectacle.

**Short-form feasibility:** One bounded question appears feasible; explanatory scope and caveats determine duration after research, with no target runtime.

**Judgment:** Finalist: strong evidence-detective story; marine adjacency is a real portfolio tradeoff, not a disqualification.
