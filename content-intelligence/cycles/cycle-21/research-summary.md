Primary research:

- [Matsumoto, Piraino & Miglietta2019, G3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6893190/) — life cycle, cyst stage, tissue participation, exploratory transcriptome. Web body inspected; preservation in web-source-inspection-5.json.
- [Piraino etal1996, Biological Bulletin](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.2307/1543022) — tissue transformation and transdifferentiation; publisher abstract only inspected. Historical Mediterranean T.nutricula is interpreted through modern taxonomy.
- [Matsumoto & Miglietta2021, GBE](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8480191/) — putative expression networks; no demonstrated causal switch. Web article body inspected; local HTML challenge is not evidence.
- [Pascual-Torner etal2022, PNAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9459311/) — comparative genomics, experimental sequence and induction; acquired full body. Candidate pathways, fragmented assembly, interspecies-distance limits; T.rubra comparison does not prove absolute inability to reverse.
- [Martell etal2016, Italian Journal of Zoology](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/11250003.2016.1203034) — life cycle and16-tentacle mature male morphology. Publisher-indexed body excerpts only, not full-paper access. Medusa morphology/counts vary.
- [Hasegawa etal2023, DNA Research](https://academic.oup.com/dnaresearch/article/30/1/dsac047/6909006) — inspected introductory body excerpt explicitly identifies experimental multiple-repeat reversal in T.dohrnii and T.sp.; no independently verified numerical count. Full article blocked.
- [Kubota2011, Biogeography](https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1050564285731346304?lang=en) — abstract/index ten reversals of unidentified Turritopsis sp.; numerical claim reserved, not transferred to dohrnii. Retained landing page is not a PDF.

Institutional marine/natural-history sources:

- [Smithsonian SERC NEMESIS species account](https://invasions.si.edu/nemesis/calnemo/species_summary/-502) — species morphology, branching hydroids, buds, regional variation and taxonomy. Actual body acquired.
- [Natural History Museum: immortal jellyfish](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/immortal-jellyfish-secret-to-cheating-death.html) — damage/starvation, cyst and predator mortality. Actual body acquired. Does not establish species-specific disease incidence or natural lifespan.

The ordinary sexual route is gametes→planula→polyp colony→budding medusa. Supported reversal route is medusa→cyst-like mass→stolon→polyps/colony→new medusae. Reorganization is not a swimming adult becoming a smaller juvenile. Some differentiated cells participate in identity changes; exact lineage/causal molecular controls remain uncertain. Repeated laboratory reversal does not demonstrate endless wild life or universal survival; colony/offspring identity must not be collapsed into a single permanently invulnerable individual.
