## What the evidence establishes

Syfret's official dispatch, London Gazette 38377, para26 identifies the 12August torpedo attack. Its 13August diary paras24–26 records subsequent air attacks and disablement. The naval liaison officer report paras12–19 describes propulsion/steering difficulty, engine-room flooding, a buckled deck and alongside towing. Hill's Ledbury proceedings p7 describes successive towing/steering arrangements, then explicitly says Penn and Bramham secured either side and brought Ohio into Valletta. The NHB brief p11 and dispatch para39 corroborate 15August arrival. NHB pp4–5 and12–13 explain fuel shortages and renewed air operations. IWM A11498 identifies actual fuel discharge into oilers Boxall and Plumleaf.

## Conflicts and exclusions

- **Hold it up:** Museum descriptions speak of buoyancy and power, but no inspected structural analysis quantifies load carried. Use the observed secured-alongside arrangement and towing/steering assistance. No lifting, hull clamping, water-exclusion mechanism, or two floating halves.
- **Which destroyers:** Hill explicitly identifies Penn and Bramham alongside in the final phase. GM1505's catalog description names Penn and Ledbury; A11498 credits Penn/Ledbury, while IWM film AYY193/19 identifies Bramham. All three participated, with changing roles. Narration and qualitative drawing say destroyers without assigning photo silhouettes specific names. Preserve contradiction rather than silently “correcting” historical catalogs. Ledbury and Rye helped tow; motor launches and local tugs also assisted.
- **Fuel quantity/type:** GM1480/GM1505 descriptions say10000tons; A11498 says11000tons; other accounts use11500. Distinguish loaded versus delivered and fuel categories; no inspected ledger resolves all quantities. Say thousands of tons of fuel. Do not describe the entire cargo as aviation gasoline.
- **Torpedo versus mine:** Early liaison/dispatch sections retain observer uncertainty. Syfret's main later narrative identifies torpedo. No specific submarine name, exact impact time, hole dimensions or impact count narrated.
- **Date:** RMG Wilkinson painting title says14August; official dispatch/history and actual photographic record support15August arrival. Do not treat an artwork title as event chronology. No exact arrival hour needed.
- **NHB error:** The brief calls Ledbury a minesweeper. It was a destroyer; do not propagate that classification. Ship class unnecessary in narration beyond generic destroyers.
- **Back broken:** Serious structural damage/buckled deck, not literally cut in half on approach. Later disposal/severing chronology excluded.
- **Significance:** Fuel contributed to aircraft operations and Malta's continued resistance. No claim one ship alone saved Malta, no precise surrender deadline, no invented civilian starvation count or casualty figure.
