This tanker reached Malta with a warship lashed to each side.

It was August nineteen forty-two. Malta was under siege, and desperately short of fuel.

Ohio was carrying thousands of tons.

A torpedo struck. Bomb attacks followed. Her engines failed, and water flooded in.

Towing her was a struggle: the damaged ship kept swinging off course.

So destroyers came alongside, securing the tanker between them, while other vessels helped with the tow.

On August fifteenth, Ohio entered Grand Harbour.

Her fuel was unloaded, helping Malta’s aircraft keep fighting.

The precious part of this wreck was still inside her.
