SS Ohio: The Fuel Inside the Wreck

CAPTION
Why bring warships alongside a damaged tanker? Ohio’s engines had failed and towing was difficult. Destroyers secured on both sides, with other vessels helping tow, brought the battered tanker to Malta on 15 August 1942. The fuel inside was the reason to keep going.

Archival photos: IWM GM1505—Sgt Deakin; GM1480—War Office official photographer; GM1469—Lt H. E. Cook; A11498—Lt J. E. Russell. Official UK government photographs, 1942; public domain (expired Crown copyright), via Wikimedia Commons. Cropped/reframed; no IWM endorsement implied.

Original reconstruction: ship forms, damage, towing lines and fuel/aircraft graphics are illustrative; time is compressed. Not archival footage or exact naval geometry. Synthetic narration and original synthesized sound, not wartime recordings.
