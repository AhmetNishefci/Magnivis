TITLE
Why They Scored an Own Goal—Then Defended Both Goals

DESCRIPTION
Barbados scored an own goal on purpose. Then they had to defend both goals.

In a 1994 Caribbean Cup qualifier against Grenada, winning by one goal would still eliminate Barbados. An unusual extra-time rule made a deliberate equalizer useful—and then changed what Grenada needed to do.

How did football's normal incentives end up reversed?
