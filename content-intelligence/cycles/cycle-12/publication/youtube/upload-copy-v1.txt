TITLE
Why Scientists Tipped a Floating Laboratory Upright

DESCRIPTION
Scientists deliberately tipped FLIP upright to study underwater sound. A narrow section near the surface and a broader hull deep below made it much less responsive to surface waves. It could still drift and tilt.
