The Nuclear Reactors Nobody Built

POST CAPTION
Earth had nuclear reactors roughly two billion years before humans could build them.

At Oklo, in what is now Gabon, geology concentrated uranium and groundwater helped sustain nuclear fission. Scientists uncovered the evidence in 1972.

These were natural reaction zones in rock. They produced heat, not electricity. Starts and stops are inferred from isotope evidence in one studied zone; the heating-and-water feedback is reconstructed.
