TITLE
Earth Had Nuclear Reactors Before Humans

DESCRIPTION
Earth had natural nuclear reactors roughly two billion years ago. Nobody built them.

At Oklo, in what is now Gabon, uranium-rich rock and groundwater sustained nuclear fission. In 1972, an unexpected uranium-235 abundance helped reveal what had happened.

How could geology create the conditions for a nuclear chain reaction? These reaction zones produced heat, not electricity. Starts and stops inferred from one studied zone are a reconstruction, not a directly observed ancient schedule.
