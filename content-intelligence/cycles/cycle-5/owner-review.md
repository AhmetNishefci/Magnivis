## Exact final narration

These patterns are drawn by vibration.

Scatter sand on a metal plate, then make it vibrate at a resonant frequency.

The moving regions bounce the grains away.

But some lines stay still. They're called nodes.

Sand gathers there, tracing the plate's pattern of motion.

Change to a different vibration mode, and a different pattern can appear.

The shape also depends on the plate and how it's held.

So the sand isn't showing where the plate moves most. It's revealing where it doesn't move.
