TITLE
Why vibration draws patterns in sand

DESCRIPTION
Sand on a vibrating plate gathers near the lines that stay still. These are nodes: the moving regions bounce grains away, leaving a pattern of low motion. Different vibration modes can produce different patterns; the plate and how it is held matter.

Original illustrative animation with synthetic narration. Grain paths and plate motion are qualitative; the tones are not calibrated to the displayed patterns.
