# MAGNIVIS — CYCLE #22 V3 VISUAL REDESIGN

## Decision

V2 is not approved for publication.

The scientific content, narration and technical execution are acceptable, but the visual representation is not yet at Magnivis quality.

The current female anglerfish appears too geometric, with a near-spherical body, simplified triangular fins and insufficient recognizable biological detail. The male also lacks visual presence.

This is a visual-quality issue, not a request for another minor camera adjustment.

## Instructions

Read AGENTS.md and the relevant canonical Magnivis documents. Verify the repository, current branch, working tree, V2 media identity and existing review evidence.

Preserve V1 and V2 masters and their evidence.

## Main objective

Redesign the fish artwork so the viewer immediately recognizes an extraordinary deep-sea organism, rather than a simple procedural diagram.

Use scientifically grounded reference material for Cryptopsaras couesii.

### Required improvements

1. **Female anatomy:** Replace the primitive spherical silhouette with a substantially more convincing species-informed design. Improve body shape, mouth, teeth, eye, skin surface, fins, lure and three caruncles. Use organic contours, believable shading and appropriate anatomical proportions.

2. **Male anatomy:** Give the male recognizable anatomical detail, especially its head, eyes and attachment structures. Make it readable during the encounter and attachment sequences.

3. **Cinematic appearance:** Improve lighting, atmospheric depth, texture, silhouettes, camera composition and animation. The result should look like polished biological filmmaking, not an abstract vector presentation.

4. **Attachment:** Make the male's jaws visibly connect to the female. Ensure the action is legible at mobile scale and that subsequent fusion clearly develops at the same anatomical location.

5. **Internal explanation:** Retain the scientifically qualified tissue and circulation cutaway, but integrate it naturally with the redesigned organisms. The viewer should understand the connection without losing spatial context.

6. **Ending:** Deliver a genuinely striking final image of the female with the attached male. Avoid reusing an almost unchanged static wide shot.

## Creative requirement

Do not solve this by merely adding more particles, labels, glow, camera movement or minor geometric detail to the existing models.

The underlying visual design must improve materially.

If the existing rendering pipeline cannot achieve convincing organic anatomy, investigate a different original-artwork or rendering approach compatible with the repository, asset rights and reproducibility requirements.

Do not introduce unlicensed third-party imagery or falsely present generated reconstruction as authentic deep-sea footage.
