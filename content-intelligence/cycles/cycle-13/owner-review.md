This is a proposed route, not an approved script or mandatory scene template.

**WHY MAGNIVIS SELECTED IT** — Immediate place-and-distance surprise supports a clear causal explanation and a meaningful ecological consequence. Apollo 12 has stronger acute human stakes; the person-switch experiment has stronger direct self-relevance; octopus RNA editing offers biological wonder. **MATERIAL RISK** — A seismic seiche is not an ocean tsunami reaching Nevada. NPS describes roughly ten-inch waves and minor disturbance in this event; no giant wave, near-extinction claim, underground ocean connection, current fish count or invented footage. General seiche physics does not establish an exact cave resonance model. If research materially undermines the promise, return to discovery and renewed Premise Review.

## Proposed hooks and first-frame concepts

Recommended: **“An earthquake thousands of miles away made waves in this Nevada desert cave. How?”**
