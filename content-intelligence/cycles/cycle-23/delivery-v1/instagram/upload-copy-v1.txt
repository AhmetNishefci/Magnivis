One engineer. Two devastated cities. A lifetime of witness.

Tsutomu Yamaguchi survived Hiroshima on August 6, 1945. Severely burned, he left on August 7 and reached Nagasaki on August 8. He survived the second atomic bombing there on August 9. His wife and infant son survived Nagasaki too.

Nagasaki acknowledged his exposure in both cities in 2009. He was not the only double survivor. He lived until 2010, aged 93, and later urged nuclear abolition.

The human cost extended far beyond this one extraordinary life.
