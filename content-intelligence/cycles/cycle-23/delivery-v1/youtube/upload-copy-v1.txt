TITLE
He Survived Two Atomic Bombs

DESCRIPTION
He survived Hiroshima. Three days later, he survived Nagasaki.

Tsutomu Yamaguchi was a Mitsubishi shipbuilding engineer in Hiroshima on business on August 6, 1945. Severely burned, he left by train on August 7 and reached Nagasaki on August 8. He survived the second bombing there on August 9.

In 2009, Nagasaki officially acknowledged his exposure in both cities. He was not the only double survivor. He lived until 2010, aged 93, and later advocated nuclear abolition.

The bombings killed tens of thousands and caused lasting suffering.
