He Survived Two Atomic Bombs

CAPTION
He survived Hiroshima. Then he returned home to Nagasaki.

Mitsubishi engineer Tsutomu Yamaguchi was in Hiroshima on business when the atomic bomb struck on August 6, 1945. Severely burned, he departed by train on August 7 and arrived in Nagasaki on August 8. On August 9, he survived the second atomic bombing.

In 2009, Nagasaki officially acknowledged his exposure in both cities. He was not the only double survivor. He died in 2010, aged 93, after becoming an advocate for nuclear abolition.

His survival is extraordinary. The bombings also killed tens of thousands and left lasting suffering.
