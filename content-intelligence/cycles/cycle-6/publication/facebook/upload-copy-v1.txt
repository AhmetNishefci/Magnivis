Why a Hotter Pan Can Make Water Last Longer

POST CAPTION
Why can a hotter pan make a drop of water last longer?

Rapid vapor formation beneath the drop creates a cushion, reducing direct liquid–metal contact. Heat still crosses that gas layer, but less direct contact can let the drop persist longer.

This is conditional: the surface and drop matter, and more heat above the transition can increase evaporation again.
