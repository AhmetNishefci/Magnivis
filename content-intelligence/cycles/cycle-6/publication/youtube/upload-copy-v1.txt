TITLE
Why a Hotter Pan Can Make Water Last Longer

DESCRIPTION
Why can a hotter pan make a drop of water last longer?

In the Leidenfrost effect, rapid vapor formation beneath the drop creates a cushion. The liquid loses direct contact with the metal, so heat must cross a poorly conducting gas layer.

This is conditional: the surface and drop matter. Hotter does not always mean a longer lifetime; above the transition, more heat can speed evaporation again.
