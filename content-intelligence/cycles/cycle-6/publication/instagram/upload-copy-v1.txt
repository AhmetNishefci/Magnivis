Why a Hotter Pan Can Make Water Last Longer

POST CAPTION
Why can a hotter pan make a drop of water last longer?

The answer lies beneath the drop: a vapor cushion reduces direct contact with the metal, so heat must cross the gas layer. This is the Leidenfrost effect.

It depends on the surface and drop. Hotter does not always mean longer; above the transition, extra heat can speed evaporation again.
