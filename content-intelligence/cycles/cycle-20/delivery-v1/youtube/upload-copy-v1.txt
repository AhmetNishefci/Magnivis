TITLE
Why You Usually Can’t Tickle Yourself

DESCRIPTION
Why can someone else's touch make you laugh, while your own usually doesn't? Your nervous system can anticipate the sensory consequences of your movements. That prediction can make some self-produced touch feel weaker.

Classic self-touch experiments used light touch, not full-on tickle laughter. Prediction is one important clue, not the whole explanation; people differ. Delayed robotic self-touch initially felt ticklier, but repeated exposure changed the comparison.

Original illustrative animation; prediction metaphor, not neural recordings. Synthetic narration; original synthesized sound.

Research: Blakemore et al. (1998); Kilteni & Ehrsson (2022); Kilteni et al. (2019); Proelss et al. (2022); Kilteni (2025 review).
