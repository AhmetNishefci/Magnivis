TITLE
No Brain. Yet a Touch Can Leave a Fading Memory.

DESCRIPTION
A Venus flytrap has no brain. A brief stimulus can still leave information behind in the leaf. Watch how a recent signal can build on that fading response—and why a longer delay changes the outcome.

“Memory” here means a temporary biochemical state, not conscious recollection. Touches, electrical signals and calcium changes are different; responses depend on stimulus and timing.

Original animated botanical reconstructions with AI-generated tissue texture and synthetic narration. Calcium responses, electrical cues and closing examples are illustrative, not naturally visible fluorescence or measured activity; timing is compressed. Research footage is a cropped Burri S1 first-deflection excerpt, already slowed in the source, with a held ending—not continuous waiting data.
