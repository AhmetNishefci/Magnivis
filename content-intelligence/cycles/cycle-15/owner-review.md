# AHMET — CYCLE #15 PREMISE REVIEW

**Recommended winner: How does a Venus flytrap remember a touch without a brain?**

**PLANNED VIDEO:** The plant keeps a fading biochemical record of stimulation. A later signal can build on what remains and change whether the trap closes. “Remember” describes retained information in a chemical state, not conscious recollection.

**WHY A COLD VIEWER MAY CARE:** A familiar plant appears to use information about something that already happened. Where could that information live without neurons? The curiosity survives without cinematic execution or the exaggerated claim that a plant thinks numbers.

**EXPECTED PAYOFF:** Understand the sequence from sensory-hair stimulation to electrical signaling and a temporary calcium rise, its decay, and the association between accumulated calcium and closure. The important idea is that a biological system can retain and combine recent signals without a brain.

**INTENDED STORY:** Observe the different responses to closely spaced and delayed brief stimuli; reveal the fading calcium trace behind the difference; return to the trap response as a threshold process. The single-movement/two-electrical-signal exception belongs in the explanation. This is an explanatory route, not final narration, a timed storyboard or approved CreativeDirection.

**TRUTH / EVIDENCE STATUS:** Preliminary evidence is strong enough for premise feasibility. [NIBB’s research-team account](https://www.nibb.ac.jp/en/pressroom/news/2020/10/06.html) describes calcium-sensor experiments; [Würzburg’s account](https://www.uni-wuerzburg.de/en/news-and-events/news/detail/news/how-the-venus-flytrap-counts-1/) explains decay and successive signals. These describe the same study, not independent replications. Direct access to the full Nature Plants paper failed. The inspected [PLOS Biology primary paper](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.3000740) qualifies the familiar rule: one sustained mechanical movement can produce two action potentials. Therefore, “always two touches” and an exact 30-second timer are unacceptable. No final claim verification is claimed; complete calcium-paper and causal-chain review remain downstream.

**VISUAL POTENTIAL / PRODUCTION CHALLENGE:** A living macro observation can lead into a continuous tissue cutaway, a fading explanatory calcium signal, and a close-versus-delayed comparison. The plant and changing biological state should carry the story. Natural tissue does not visibly glow: any color must be a labeled illustration or accurately identified engineered-reporter imaging. Anatomy, timing and signal-to-motion meaning need review; a schematic dashboard with calcium numbers would underserve the premise. No production assets were acquired or generated.
