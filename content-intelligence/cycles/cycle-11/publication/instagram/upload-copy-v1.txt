To defend himself, he had to prove the supposed masterpiece was a fake.

Han van Meegeren’s confession challenged paintings experts had accepted as Vermeers. Investigators found evidence of modern synthetic resin and separate crack networks in old and newer paint layers. An old canvas did not make the painting an old master.

The collaboration case fell away. The fraud did not: he was convicted of fraud and forgery in 1947 and sentenced to one year.
