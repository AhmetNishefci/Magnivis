# Cycle11 publication sources and truth boundaries

- Froentjes and de Wild, De natuurwetenschappelijke bewijsvoering in het proces Van Meegeren (1949)
  https://chg.kncv.nl/l/library/download/urn:uuid:90babdb6-20d4-4663-8f69-edf7cb917412/chw-1949---17--proces-van-meegeren--ocr.compressed--1-.pdf?ext=.pdf&format=save_to_disk
  Report by two court-appointed examiners, contemporary to the investigation. Dutch OCR checked against parsed passages; summaries translated, no purported courtroom transcript.
  Limits: Methods and observations belong to the eight examined paintings; do not generalize every fake or reproduce later simplified X-ray claims. Report combines van Meegeren’s explanation with experimental confirmation; pigment tests alone did not date these paintings. Some color reactions alone were insufficient; isolated binder, optical comparison, cross-sections and material comparisons formed the evidence.

- Theodore Rousseau, The Stylistic Detection of Forgeries, Bulletin26(6),1968
  https://resources.metmuseum.org/resources/metpublications/pdf/Art_Forgery_The_Metropolitan_Museum_of_Art_Bulletin_v_26_no_6_February_1968.pdf
  Art historian who interrogated intermediary Miedl; specialist retrospective account. His own interrogation is participant-linked; other reconstruction is later interpretation.
  Limits: 1968 retrospective account is not a contemporaneous interrogation transcript. Psychological motives, exact dialogue, cobalt-blue shortcut and universal statements not adopted.

- Anna Tummers, The fingerprint of an old master (doctoral thesis,2009)
  https://pure.uva.nl/ws/files/866858/68241_thesis02.pdf
  Footnoted specialist research separating period criticism, material evidence, later claims and disputed retrospection.
  Limits: Secondary legal description uses treason broadly; narration instead says suspicion of collaboration. Painting during the trial denotes the extended1945–47 proceedings, not proven painting in1947courtroom. Some experts rejected the work; never claim unanimous acceptance.

- Een jaar voor Han v. Meegeren (12November1947)
  https://www.nrc.nl/nieuws/1947/11/12/een-jaar-voor-han-v-meegeren-schilderijen-worden-teruggegeven-bepaalt-de-amsterdamse-rechtbank-in-haar-vonnis-kb_000062076-a2071221
  Same-day court reporting quotes verdict descriptions. Original court judgment not acquired; archive OCR is reporting rather than certified transcript.
  Limits: OCR names/punctuation may be imperfect; exact dialogue excluded. Emmaus itself was time-barred; do not say every forgery was a count in conviction.

- Art: The Price of Forgery (18November1946)
  https://time.com/archive/6773737/art-the-price-of-forgery/
  Near-contemporary public reporting on changing legal position, cross-checked with later verdict coverage.
  Limits: Reported legal status, not original dismissal order; patriotic self-presentation is his claim, not established motive.

- Art: Truth & Consequences (24November1947)
  https://time.com/archive/6823954/art-truth-consequences/
  Contemporary trial reporting corroborates accusation, laboratory evidence and one-year sentence.
  Limits: Unrelated promotional FAQ preceding the actual article excluded; identity anchored to historical body and date. Reported dialogue, money, authority count and psychological/motive claims excluded.

- Han van Meegeren schildert in1945 als demonstratie van zijn kunnen
  https://www.nationaalarchief.nl/onderzoeken/fotocollectie/af58658a-d0b4-102d-bcf8-003048976d84
  Institutional metadata for an actual period photograph of demonstration painting.
  Limits: Metadata supports a1945demonstration, not an exact start/end date or courtroom venue. Photograph inspected via repository-independent archive page; no production use planned.

Emmaus is not Adulteress sold to Göring. No courtroom painting demonstration, execution deadline, invented dialogue or heroic motive. Collaboration suspicion and fraud conviction remain distinct. Modern explanatory graphics are not historical footage. Source inspection evidence remains in the original cycle research records.
