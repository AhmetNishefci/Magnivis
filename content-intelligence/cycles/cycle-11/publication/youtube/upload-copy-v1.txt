TITLE
His Defense: The Vermeer Was His Own Forgery

DESCRIPTION
His defense was that the supposed masterpiece was his own forgery.

In 1945, Han van Meegeren was arrested on suspicion of collaboration after a supposed Vermeer reached Hermann Göring through an art dealer. Technical evidence supported his confession: old supports did not make modern resin-based paint centuries old. The collaboration case fell away; in 1947 he was convicted of fraud and forgery and sentenced to one year.

The Supper at Emmaus shown here illustrates earlier expert acceptance. It is not the painting sold to Göring. No courtroom painting demonstration or execution deadline is claimed.
