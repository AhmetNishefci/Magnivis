The existing WORKFLOW-V3 production/QA stage and all three owner gates remain unchanged. ASSET-LICENSES separately records V2 graphic provenance. Historical discovery receipts bind WORKFLOW-V3 and CREATIVE-DIRECTION bytes; a first full check detected that amendments would break replay. Their exact bytes were restored, and the same actionable guidance is owned by AGENTS/PRODUCTION-PLANS instead. No historical receipt was rewritten or digest bypassed, and no document-snapshot framework was needed.

Every meaningful correction must fix the artifact, assess a reusable process lesson, integrate that lesson into the appropriate existing canonical practice, preserve evidence/reasoning and apply it in later production. Repeated corrections trigger investigation without another owner request. No new ledger, schema, score, infrastructure or owner approval gate was added.

Future sessions already read AGENTS and WORKFLOW-V3 before changes. AGENTS now requires reading applicable master corrections from the existing journal history before direction/production and recording application/non-generalization in cycle evidence. The production-plan guidance now requires the normal pre-handoff review to compare direction promises with the actual complete rendered sequence, record concrete weak stretches and internally revise clear actionable failures. This is discoverable session behavior, not a background daemon or a runtime guarantee.

Recurrence can be detected by checking whether attribution/discovery/mechanism developments appear visually, whether adjacent structures repeat without explanatory purpose, whether text is a shortcut, and whether opening/payoff fulfill the promise for an unfamiliar viewer. The review must cite actual time/scene evidence and honest inspection limits. Similarity and stillness remain legitimate; no cut/motion quotas, mandatory suspense, reenactment or fixed aesthetic.
