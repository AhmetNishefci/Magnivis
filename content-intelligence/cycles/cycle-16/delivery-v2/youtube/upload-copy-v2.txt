TITLE
How NASA Worked Around Voyager 1’s Broken Memory

DESCRIPTION
Voyager 1 was still sending signals—but its data was unreadable. NASA engineers split and relocated affected code into working memory, then updated its references. The failed chip stayed failed.

Readable engineering data returned on April 20, 2024. Science data followed later.

Spacecraft imagery: Courtesy NASA/JPL-Caltech, artist concepts PIA17049 and PIA17036, cropped/reframed. Memory, radio-path and data graphics are simplified illustrations, not actual hardware layouts or telemetry. Synthetic narration and original synthesized sound; not spacecraft recordings.
