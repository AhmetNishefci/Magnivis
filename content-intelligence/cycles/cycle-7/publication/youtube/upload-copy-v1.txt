TITLE
How Can Light Reveal an Obstacle Without Being Absorbed or Reflected?

DESCRIPTION
How can light reveal an obstacle without being absorbed or reflected by it?

One photon. Two possible paths. In a carefully aligned setup, their contributions cancel at one output, keeping that detector dark.

An absorber blocks a path. Sometimes it absorbs the photon. But in some trials, the formerly dark detector clicks: the obstacle is detected without absorbing that photon. Blocking a possible path removed the cancellation.

This is interaction-free measurement. It is probabilistic: other trials absorb the photon or give no clear answer. The animation shows an ideal model, not recorded laboratory footage; the paths are quantum possibilities, not two half-photons.
