## Revised exact narration

Pick a die. Higher number wins. Whatever you choose, I can pick one with better odds.

Every face has an equal chance in this puzzle, and neither roll affects the other.

Yellow always rolls three. Orange has four twos and two sixes. Yellow wins against four faces out of six: two-thirds.

But blue has four fours, so it has better odds against yellow.

Switch to blue? Green’s fives beat every blue face. Its ones still beat blue’s zeros.

Then orange counters green: its sixes beat everything, and its twos beat green’s ones.

And yellow still counters orange.

Every reply shown has those same two-thirds odds. That’s an advantage, not a guaranteed win.

The advantages form a circle. There’s no best die in this set—just a better matchup.
