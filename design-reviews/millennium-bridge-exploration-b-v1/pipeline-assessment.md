## A–F assessment

A. Procedural rendering can reach the required quality for precise structural displacement, damping response, restrained temporal witnesses, typography and disclosure. Rich human anatomy/materials/environment will demand a more sophisticated rig/rendering effort than the current SVG figures.

B. Opening human/environment, close shoe/material detail, crowd scale and hardware macro materially benefit from richer original assets, directional lighting, texture, depth of field and compositing. These improve separation, physical credibility and shot variety rather than add novelty.

C. Add only a small source-asset contract for this exploration: immutable raster bytes, SHA-256, dimensions, origin/tool/prompt/access metadata, usage status and composition/crop parameters. No new CMS, generic scene factory or production architecture rewrite. Existing Remotion composition can later consume frozen assets behind scene-specific layers.

D. Generated asset creation is not byte deterministic. Freeze exact original tool outputs and record their hashes plus actual generation metadata. Re-prompting produces a new asset/revision requiring review. Downstream resizing/cropping, compositing, font outlines, motion witnesses, manifests and PNG output remain deterministic and pinned. Preserve unknown tool/model/seed fields as null rather than inventing provider IDs. Prompt text and source bytes are durable; prompt reruns cannot promise old hashes.

E. Owner permits exploring generated original imagery. Use the built-in imagegen tool, without external photos/footage or named-artist copying. Generated views are illustrative synthetic reconstructions, not opening-day photographs or research evidence. Provider output terms are recorded with access date; they do not certify uniqueness or legal copyrightability. Review-only usage now; final asset selection, anatomy/hardware accuracy and publication remain future human gates.

F. Recommend hybrid production: physically rich original environment/human/material layers plus exact procedural mechanisms and restrained compositing. Five static concepts prove art direction only, not a sustained animation rig. A later authorized production prototype must test segmentation/parallax, gait, geometry continuity and mechanism causality before committing to this pipeline. No AI-image slideshow or simple zoom-only substitute is proposed.
