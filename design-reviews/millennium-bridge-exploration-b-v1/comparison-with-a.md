# Material comparison: Exploration A → B

A remains intact and is owner-rejected for visual execution only. Its exact manifest, five PNG hashes and editorial binding are captured in `../decisions/millennium-bridge-exploration-a-rejection-v1.json`. Research and editorial approval are unchanged.

| Owner concern | B response | Remaining design-review question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Procedural engineering explainer | Five cinematographic shot scales with textured original imagery, sparse original compositing | Do the images and proposed motion sustain a documentary feeling? |
| Mannequin figures | Anatomical clothed person, bent knees, weight-bearing shoes, asymmetric corrective posture | The still is expressive; controlled gait/weight transfer still needs a motion prototype |
| Dark empty world | Bright silver deck, warm sky, river texture, London depth and populated wide shot | Does tonal separation preserve Magnivis atmosphere without losing legibility? |
| Weak first image | Foreground person fills the image; dramatic perspective and searching foot introduce instability | Does a static stance communicate moving ground immediately enough? Actual causal order needs animation |
| Correction unclear | Low camera, lean, stretched arm and displaced foot; near-ground second shot isolates contact | Generated anatomy must be checked again when posed/animated |
| Contact rings diagrammatic | Rings removed; contact is carried by the sole/metal interface | Very faint exposure witnesses remain illustrative rather than measured force graphics |
| Crowd/coherence too similar | Longitudinal crowd reveal versus overhead diagonal phase study | Sparse local trails suggest selected relationships, not measured historical phases |
| Damping too subtle | Large integrated cylinder, rod and anchors with a shrinking motion witness | A still cannot establish dissipation; piston and decaying structural motion must carry the future payoff |
| Cohesion costs variety | Human-scale → macro → wide → overhead → hardware detail | Palette, light and textured metal unify distinct scales; exact geometry is not identical across generated shots |
| Engineering presentation | Cinematic synthetic environment and shot construction, no giant labels or panels | Avoid an image slideshow: future layer separation/rigged action is a necessary technical gate |

The B source pipeline materially raises illustration quality but does not yet solve animated continuity. It is intentionally a design exploration, not a claim that five flattened sources can become a convincing full production through parallax alone. Scientific meaning and approved narration remain exactly as before. No new historical evidence comes from the images.
